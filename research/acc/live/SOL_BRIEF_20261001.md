# Brief for Sol 6.1: Andrews–Curtis challenge, our best experiment ideas (1 Oct 2026, 02:35Z)

**Context.** SAIR Andrews–Curtis Discovery Track (github.com/SAIRcompetition/Andrews-Curtis). 10,115 balanced two-generator presentations, scored twice:
AC (moves ac-r2-v1: invert r_i; r_i <- r_i r_j^±1; conjugate r_i by x^±1 or y^±1; target ((x),(y))) and Stable AC (sac-r8-v1: the same plus stabilize to rank <= 8 and
destabilize; an AC path + [destab r1, destab r0] is always a Stable path, +2 moves). Score per row: first solve 1.0, beat 1.0, tie 2^-k (k = teams holding the record).
2,672 rows are unsolved on the board, 550 of them exactly Miller–Schupp MS(n,w) = <x,y | x^-1 y^n x y^-(n+1), x^-1 w>.
Our team ("Mad Hatters"): AC rank #15 (3.99 points), Stable #12 (9.15; #13 is 0.26 behind). The owner's goals: hold and raise rank (ties count), and land a real first solve.

**What we learned tonight (data, not opinion)**
1. The leader's Stable line = its AC line + 2 on 98.7% of its rows. A (y,x)-finish on a road at or 1 over its AC record ties or beats its Stable record. This gave 4 ties in 75 minutes.
2. The leader posts nightly batches (00:00-04:00Z). Records of 40 or under almost never move (8 beats); long rows (>150) keep moving.
3. MS "lucky n": the pump leaves r1 carrying a y-run of n-2 next to x. When w has a y-run of that length next to x, records break the growth trend
   (7 of 15 solved cases vs 2 of 51 otherwise). The n=4 routes rely on that coincidence; it fails at n=3.
4. The official metadata groups the 550 open MS rows into 261 known AC-equivalence classes (14_1 has 36 rows, 15_1 22, length-19 singletons...).
   All our first-solve candidates are in open classes: the literature's hard frontier.
5. Helper generators in Stable (stabilize mid-route) did not save moves in exact window tests: a helper starts as a bare letter and nothing multiplies a relator by itself.
6. Every verified improvement today came from joining saved roads (splicing two lineages), with fresh bridges anchored on them; none came from fresh search alone.
7. Exact twins: 1,207 groups of rows are the same presentation up to symmetry (x<->y, inversions, relator swap/rotation); routes transfer at a small fixed cost.

**The experiment ideas (we can run one or two per hour on our strongest model)**
- **A. Level-map extrapolation (session 1), current top pick.** Length-19 singleton MS(4,w) rows ac-02598 (w = y^2 x^2 y x^-2) and ac-01449 (w = x^-2 y^-2 x^2 y).
  Their n=3 siblings ac-00764 and ac-06036 are also unsolved (classes 17_16, 17_5); ac-00766 (n=2) is solved at 33. Plan: exact optimal routes at n=2/3 (exact bidirectional
  all-moves search, validated: MS(1)=7, MS(2)=16, MS(3)=34 for w = YXYXyx), read the level map (the n-1 route plus an inserted block), build n=4, repair with an
  exact depth-9 ball around the goal at relator cap 24.
- **B. Template transfer across w at fixed n (UPG-H, running).** Carry our solved lucky n=4 route (ac-08510, 35 moves; phases: collapse r0 against the y^n pump with
  n-1 conjugations, build r0 = (XY)^(n+1) Y and r1 = y (yx)^n, tail of n moves) to unsolved lucky n=4 rows ac-09310, ac-07430, ac-08606 (classes 15_10/15_11).
- **C. Lift n -> n+1 by inserted moves (Bob).** In the official training paths, inserting one move lifts the n path to n+1 in 45 of 224 certified pairs.
  Running over all scored MS rows; no hits yet.
- **D. Paid class-collision joins (Astra).** Index conjugacy classes of ordered relator pairs over a 70,818-state graph of verified routes; connect class
  collisions with fully paid alignment moves. Rows ac-00034/00028/00003 (3-9 moves over their records).
- **E. (y,x) finish everywhere (all).** Any road at or 1-2 over an AC record on the leader's lazy-Stable rows; splice-then-(y,x) on new lineages.
