## 2. Structure found

- **Pulse opening, MS(n, YYXYYXyyx):** `6 10 12` + `9`x(n-3) + `3 9 7 3 6 12 4` (n+9 moves) sends every n to
  r0 = Y^(n-2) x y^(n-1) XYX with **r1 = yyxYYXYX fixed for all n** (checked n=5..10). Found by comparing the n=5 and
  n=6 beam paths ("difference of two"): they differ by one extra move 9.
- **Pump, same family:** `8 8 12 12` + rounds of `5 12 12` shrink r1 one letter per round; clean at odd n, leaves xYX at even n.
- **Parity wall, MS(n, YYXXYx):** odd n drops by 2 (17, 21, 25 for n=5,7,9), even n never drops (n=4,6,8,10). Every
  search so far, ours and the team's, sees the same split.
- **n+1 reduces into n:** n=6 greedy paths end with the n=5 path's moves (46 and 108 common tail moves), so a level step
  exists; no exact level-to-level step of <= 14 moves (two-sided BFS 7+7, cap 26).
- **MCSC records rise in pairs** (n=5,6 / 7,8 / 9,10) in all three families: a 2-step construction in n.

- The order-6 map (x->[[0,-1],[1,1]]) has blind spots (both lines read 0 at total 17 on MS(5), not solved). Use Sanov.
- **Vacuum = scale-free point:** x, y are parabolic (eigenvalue 1, vectors merged, pure shear); character (2,2,6), kappa 18 (not 0).
- **On every solved route both lines keep their eigenvectors until the last 0-7 moves, then lose them together.**
  No recorded unsolved descent ever loses one.
- **kappa = tr[rho(r0), rho(r1)] is frozen under moves 0-5** (Fricke; confirmed by session 1, 300 random states, and here
  0 of 557 recorded moves); conjugations change it (1032 of 1037). Same fact as comm_len invariance.
- **Unsolved MS(n, YYXXYx) starts sit in a kappa pit:** kappa-keyed beams keep the start as best (beam-bounded); every
  length-first descent raises the kappa gap (n=8: 18.6 -> 35.8). On the n=5 descent r1 closes (Sanov 12.9 -> 8.6) while
  r0 gets worse (10.3 -> 13.0): **r0 is the wall.**
- Snell: pooled speed is slow at total 10-24 (0.02-0.13 letters/move) and fast at 25-34 (0.46-1.0) and 5-9 (1.7):
  the lifeguard route climbs into the fast band, then collapses.
