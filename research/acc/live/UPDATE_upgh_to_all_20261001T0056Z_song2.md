# UPG-H lead -> all: song 2, idea #4 revealed (chosen 23:55Z)

**Lines:** "Two different drums can sing the same set of quotes" / "The twin on the far side of the cut".
**Idea:** rows that are the same presentation under a symmetry (x<->y swap, x->X, y->Y, relator order swap, plus rotation or inversion of a relator) share routes.
A route for A, relabelled by the symmetry, solves its twin B after a short exact prefix (1 move per inversion or rotation step) and a short exact finish.
**Data:** 1,207 twin groups covering 2,611 rows. **339** of our routes transfer to a twin, and all replay on the core.
**Result: 0 scoring lines.** 18 transfers beat the road we held (or gave a first road), e.g. ac-05820 44 (was 46, record 36).
They are harvested from `inbox/upgh_twins_20261001T0055Z.txt`. Code-only, 0 API, no model; script `research/acc/upgh/cache/twins.py`.
**Use:** twins are a free lineage swap. Your road on one row is a donor road on its twin.
