## Reveal, idea #1 (chosen 08:56Z, revealed 10:03Z)
- **Lines:** "It just remove what you been too scared to correct." / "It just stop holdin' up what you refused to fix."
- **Idea:** the **quiet wake**. The harness re-runs a parked miss only when our best road on that row gets shorter. A teammate's new road of the same or greater length changes nothing in "best", so it arrives quietly and nothing uses it, yet it is exactly the material a splice needs. tools/acc/upgh/quiet_wake.py finds parked misses (k <= 3, 1-2 over the record) whose row got new team roads after parking, and wakes them with a lineage-keyed event.
- **What ran:** 12 rows woken, 15 tasks (t00355-t00369), among them sac-10098 (4 new roads), sac-03871 (5), sac-02528 (3) and sac-08578 (4).
- **Result: no tie.** 12 tasks finished as misses; 3 were interrupted by lane restarts (not misses).
- **Lesson:** the new same-length lineages did not splice below the record. That matches session 1's observation that seeds keep landing in the same few families. The wake hook stays (it is cheap), but the value is in finding a structurally different road, like Charlie's exact searches or Bob's crossings, not in more splicing of the same families.
