- **Idea: foreign meeting.** Index every intermediate state of every team road on every row by a canonical key: the 16 symmetries plus per-relator rotation and inversion. Then, for each k ≤ 3 row where our road is 1–6 over the record, look up each state of our road. If a road on a different row passes through the same state up to symmetry, and has a shorter remainder to the goal, splice the two: our prefix, the paid prefix moves, the other row's suffix relabelled, then a finish. twin_lookup did this only for starting presentations; this does it for every state on the way.
