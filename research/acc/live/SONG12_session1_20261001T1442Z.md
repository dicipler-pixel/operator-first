# SONG 12 (session 1): "People are the relations"

**Line:** "the real ones stay linked even when they ain't seen." **Picked:** 13:41Z.

**Idea:** a row nobody has solved may still be linked to a road someone else already walked. If any state reachable from an open row's start lies on any known road to (x,y), on any row and in any of its 16 symmetric images, then that road finishes the open row. That would be a first solve, made from other people's work.

**Build:** `roadset.py` replays 8,090 verified roads (the team pool, our audit harvest and roads_latest). It collects every intermediate state, plus 16 symmetric images (letter flips, x<->y, relator swap), into a sorted acc_disk set of 1,009,640 states. Then each open row runs `acc_disk bfs ... --meet roads.set`. Sanity check: ac-02902 meets its own road at layer 1.

**Result (exact, finite):**
| row | cap | states | meet |
|---|---|---|---|
| ac-00738 | 26 / 28 | 15.0M / 180.9M | none (CLOSED) |
| ac-06611 | 26 (28 via the 5-move bridge) | 15.0M | none |
| ac-10099 | 26 / 28 | 9.8M / 61.4M | none (CLOSED) |
| ac-06412 | 41, depth 12 | 20,068 | none |

**Lesson:** the open basins are islands. No known road passes within their cap-28 reach. Together with song 11 (the starts are local minima), this says a road for these rows must be NEW and must climb above 28. It also says the relation must be built, not found: start from the target side, with a high cap and length-limited states, and grow the "people" until they reach the island.

**Side result:** a radius-5 symmetry-link scan of 8 unsolved starts found only the known 00738 <-> 06611 bridge.
