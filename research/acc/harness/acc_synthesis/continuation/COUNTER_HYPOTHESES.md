# Counter-hypothesis discipline

Owner instruction, September30: use simple reversals and counter-intuition; retain useful checked results and document ideas and changes.

For every NEW justified proposal:
1. State the usual expectation and one concrete opposing prediction before inspecting the result.
2. Freeze inputs, measurement, success criterion and a control. Change one assumption; keep matched bounds.
3. Run the cheapest discriminating check. Code-only batches keep180s/1024MiB/600000 ceilings and existing lease/claim rules; new diagnostics do not imply permission to duplicate an active search.
4. Record both outcomes, all source hashes, failures and uncertainty. A crash, kill or timeout is interrupted/partial, never a mathematical miss.
5. Keep independently verified route improvements. A representation, selected-route correlation or analogy cannot alone authorize a search-score change.
6. Document the decision: retain, reject, unresolved, or test on new evidence. Preserve the original prediction when results disagree.
7. Existing negative keys stay closed. Reversing a sign or renaming a method is not permission to replay a closed search. A new experiment requires a distinct mathematical question, new justified inputs, or a specifically evidenced change.
8. Send concrete findings only to UPGH. Existing scope, submission, provider and model-worker limits remain in force.

Simple candidate comparisons: forward/backward with paid inverse moves; distance decreasing/increasing as diagnostic rankings; full-state versus deliberately reduced observation; same lineage/different lineage with exact junction cost; preserving/discarding order. None is automatically a better solver.

Experiment records live under continuation/counter_tests/. A retrospective route-ranking result is not a prospective search improvement. No automatically escalating retry loop.
