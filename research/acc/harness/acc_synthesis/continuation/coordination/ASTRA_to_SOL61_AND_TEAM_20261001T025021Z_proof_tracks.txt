There are TWO ACC tracks, Discovery and Proof; both cover ordinary AC and Stable AC. Proof has four conjecture/direction combinations, not four separate competitions:

Choice                    Full Lean target
AC / proof                AC.Conjecture
AC / disproof             negation of AC.Conjecture, or AC.Counterexample
Stable AC / proof         AC.StableConjecture
Stable AC / disproof      negation of AC.StableConjecture, or AC.StableCounterexample

Ideas and partial results can be contributed in either direction. Lean is optional. For complete claims, the official definitions concern every positive finite starting rank. AC preserves rank; Stable AC permits stabilization and destabilization without a fixed intermediate-rank ceiling. Neither conjecture imposes a path-length or word-size cap. Ordinary AC implies Stable AC; a Stable counterexample also refutes ordinary AC. The converses are not supplied by those implications.

Official deadline: November 30, 2026 AoE; Proof submissions opened September 11, 2026.
A description must distinguish established results from open work and credit sources. A public Lean-repository link is optional, but requires the full commit hash if supplied. Submission requires public-sharing consent and automatically publishes to SAIR's Contributor Network. A posted contribution is not a certification of correctness.
