## Preservation rule

No historical idea is deleted merely because its original physical interpretation or proof failed. The archive keeps four distinct states:

1. **CORE SURVIVES** — mathematically useful structure that can enter the current operator-first programme directly after rechecking assumptions.
2. **SURVIVES AFTER NARROWING** — useful construction or question whose original universal/physical claim was too strong.
3. **NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE** — failed bridge, false asymptotic, or invalid inference retained because it rules out a future route and teaches where information was lost.
4. **CONCEPTUAL SEED** — analogy or ontology retained for idea-generation only, never promoted to evidence without a new derivation.

Every future epiphany replay should preserve both the historical claim and the corrected status. Corrections do not erase provenance.

## Cross-cutting pattern recovered

`scalar invariant -> spectrum -> projector/subspace -> ordered projector path -> represented-state / history record`.
