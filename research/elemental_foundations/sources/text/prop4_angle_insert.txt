                                                   Abstract
       The paper already computes Φ∞ = 1 − (u1·v1 )2 in five places without naming the quantity. It is
       sin2 of the principal angle between the transport’s left and right dominant singular directions.
       Naming it costs one lemma and upgrades two propositions: the value of a polynomial continued
       fraction becomes the tangent of that angle, and the Kronecker composition law becomes the
       statement that direction cosines multiply. Figure 8’s Φ scale becomes an angle scale on [0◦ , 90◦ ].
       Nothing in the paper changes; the same numbers acquire a geometric name. All claims verified
       to 60 digits; script prop4-angle-01.

    The identity Φ = 1 − (u1 ·v1 )2 is the one already used throughout; the content of the lemma is only

    secures.

6    Where this goes, and what it does not change
    • Lemma 1: new, immediately before Proposition 3 in Section 9.1.
    • Proposition 1: replaces the second sentence of Proposition 4, Section 10.1.
    • The an → ∞ remark: immediately after it. This is a strengthening, not a correction — the
      existing statement already carries the hypothesis.
    • Proposition 2: a corollary to Proposition 3, Section 9.1.
    • Figure 8: caption sentence, plus the Apéry row.
    • Summary item 7 gains the tangent statement; item 6 gains the cosine law.
    • Appendix: prop4-angle-01 as a fifteenth listing.
No numerical value in the paper changes. No proof is corrected. Every quantity in the tables above
is one the paper already reports; what is new is the name of the quantity and the two composition
laws that follow once it has one.
One structural remark worth making in the Summary: the same invariant appears in three other-
wise unrelated sectors of this work — the principal angle here, the cos2 principal angles that index
a boundary channel census, P  and the sin2 form of the refraction law. The Hilbert–Schmidt distance
between two projectors is 2 i sin2 θi by construction, so any diagnostic built from projector dis-
tances is natively sin2 -valued in principal angles. That is not a coincidence between the sectors; it
is the same measurement performed on different pairs of subspaces.
