    Abstract
A recurring proposal in cosmology holds that there is no absolute
reference of size: a universe of shrinking matter in static space is
claimed to be observationally equivalent to fixed matter in expanding
space. We take the strongest version of that proposal seriously,
stress-test it, and then ask the question it opens: if matter has no
absolute scale, what is matter? Five results follow. (1) The
proposal is well posed only as a closed units dictionary, and the
dictionary closes uniquely: holding \(\hbar\), \(c\) and \(\alpha\) fixed, atomic redshift in static
space forces \(\ell,t\propto(1+z)\) and
\(m\propto(1+z)^{-1}\) — Wetterich’s
direction — because the Bohr rod \(a_0
m=\hbar/(\alpha c)\) makes size and mass scale oppositely;
assignments scaling them together miss by four powers of \((1+z)\). (2) Once closed, the theory
bifurcates into a pure reframing (prediction set identical to the
standard one) or an environment-dependent scaling (a scalar–tensor
theory facing the lunar-laser, equivalence-principle and clock walls).
(3) Transplanted into an operator framework the no-absolute-scale demand
becomes a theorem with a measured boundary: difference observables are
exactly invariant under \(K\mapsto
K+cI\), spectral projectors, principal angles and the infrared
boundary census are exactly invariant under \(K\mapsto cK\), and the single failure is an
ultraviolet logarithm with coefficient \(A=c/\pi^2\) per boundary point. New in
this version: the coefficient’s central-charge dependence is now
tested — an exact Majorana-chain computation at \(c=1/2\) brackets the predicted \(1/\pi^2\) from both sides and lands more
than forty times closer to it than to the \(c=1\) value; additivity of independent
sectors extends the test to a parameter-free linearity law \(A=2c/\pi^2\), the entanglement ladder
behind the coefficient is measured directly at \(c=\tfrac12\), and the dictionary of the
units argument is closed over gravity, with \(\alpha_G=Gm^2/\hbar c\) invariant. A
rescaling of the substrate can only be registered as a running, never as
a power. (4) The same framework answers the opened question: matter is a
gapped substrate wearing a boundary — gapped census finite and area-law,
directions priced, records protected — while gas is the gapless
phase: census flooded, directions free, failures non-modal, and walls
only where the gas manufactures them. Matter is classified by three
scale-free ratios, none of which carries an absolute size. (5) The
environmental branch’s one remaining confrontation is now executed with
the RAR baseline explicit: a \(G\) that
grows with emptiness predicts a negative residual–compactness dependence
at fixed acceleration and mass, while every measured sign in the single
SPARC regime that retains structural information is positive — and each
escape from the sign either collapses the mechanism to a reframing of
the acceleration relation or strands it below the \(0.106\) dex extraction floor, where it
explains nothing.


    

  


1Introduction

Every measuring instrument needs two calibrations: a zero and a span.
The zero fixes what counts as nothing; the span fixes what counts as
twice as much. A theory of measurement that cannot say where its zero
and span come from has not finished being stated.

Cosmology has carried a version of this question for sixty years.
Dicke [1] observed that
physics is invariant under a transformation of units; Hoyle and Narlikar
[2] built a
conformal theory of gravitation on the observation; Wetterich [3] made the modern
rigorous version explicit: a universe that shrinks rather than expands,
with the Planck mass and all particle masses growing and atoms shrinking
correspondingly, reproduces observation exactly, because only
dimensionless ratios — galaxy separation over atomic radius — are ever
measured. Gradenwitz [5] arrived at the same demand
independently: a static universe in which matter shrinks, redshift read
as the record of atoms that were larger when the light was emitted.
Stripped of its cosmology, that paper’s real thesis is that there is no
absolute reference of size.

This paper takes the thesis as seriously as it can be taken, in three
movements. First we stress-test the strongest version of shrinking
matter (Sec. 2): the dictionary it requires closes
uniquely, and not in the direction proposed. Second we transplant the
demand into a framework where it can be proved or refuted (Sec. 3): it becomes a theorem in three
places and fails by a logarithm in the fourth — and the coefficient of
the logarithm, predicted to carry the substrate’s central charge, has
now been tested at a second central charge and does (Sec. 3.4). Third, we let the demand answer the
question it raises (Sec. 4): if nothing about matter carries an
absolute size, then what distinguishes matter from gas, and either from
vacuum, must be statable entirely in scale-free quantities — and it is.
The paper closes with the environmental branch’s one remaining
confrontation with data, now executed rather than proposed (Sec. 5).

2The strongest version of shrinking matter

We state the proposal in its strongest form and attack it where it
can be attacked. What survives the attack is worth keeping; what does
not survive was never load-bearing.

2.1The dictionary must close, and it closes uniquely

A shrinking-matter theory is a claim about units, so it is well posed
only as a complete dictionary — and the uniqueness claimed below is
uniqueness under the stated assumptions (fixed \(c\), \(\hbar\), \(\alpha\) and the atomic redshift
condition), not over every conceivable varying-constant theory: explicit
functions \(\hbar(z)\), \(c(z)\), \(e(z)\), \(m(z)\), \(\ell(z)\), \(t(z)\), under the constraint that every
dimensionless invariant — \(\alpha=e^2/\hbar
c\), \(m_p/m_e\), the
Rydberg–second product — is unchanged. That constraint is not optional
bookkeeping; it is the entire content of the claim of observational
equivalence. This is Wetterich’s discipline [3], and any version of the proposal that
skips it is not yet a theory.


Theorem 1 (Closure). Fix \(c\) and \(\hbar\) constant and demand \(\alpha\) invariant. If redshift in static
space is atomic — emitted wavelengths at epoch \(z\) larger by \((1+z)\) — then the dictionary is forced:
\[\ell \propto (1+z),\qquad t \propto
(1+z),\qquad m \propto (1+z)^{-1},\qquad
e^2 = \alpha\hbar c \ \text{fixed}.\] All dimensionless
invariants are then unchanged: \(\alpha\) by construction, \(m_p/m_e\) because all masses carry the same
factor, and the Rydberg–second product because \({\rm Ry}=\tfrac12\alpha^2 m c^2 \propto
(1+z)^{-1}\) while atomic seconds scale as \((1+z)\).




Proof. Atomic lengths are set by the Bohr radius \(a_0=\hbar^2/(m e^2) =
\hbar/(\alpha m c)\). With \(\hbar\), \(c\), \(\alpha\) fixed, \(a_0\propto(1+z)\) forces \(m\propto(1+z)^{-1}\). Times follow lengths
through fixed \(c\). Charge follows
from \(e^2=\alpha\hbar c\). Invariance
of the listed ratios is a one-line check in each case. ◻



Masses smaller in the past, growing with cosmic time, atoms shrinking
correspondingly: this is exactly the direction of Wetterich’s universe
[3]. The closure
does not merely permit that lineage; it derives it.


Theorem 2 (Gravitational closure). Extend the
dictionary by scaling the Planck mass with all particle masses, \(M_{\rm Pl}\propto m\propto(1+z)^{-1}\).
Then \(G=\hbar c/M_{\rm Pl}^2
\propto(1+z)^{2}\), and the dimensionless gravitational coupling
\(\alpha_G=Gm^2/\hbar c\) is invariant.
The dictionary is closed over matter and gravity together, and the fork
of Sec. 2.3 becomes exact: Branch 1 is \(M_{\rm Pl}=M_{\rm Pl}(z)\) alone, and
Branch 2 is precisely the statement \(M_{\rm
Pl}=M_{\rm Pl}(z,E)\) — an environmental dependence of the
gravitational scale.



This paper does not attempt an early-universe evolution; the closed
dictionary nevertheless identifies the invariants a dynamical
realization must preserve, and modern nucleosynthesis calculations for
varying masses and couplings [28, 29] are the appropriate downstream
consistency test.

Against this, consider the assignment in which mass, size, time and
charge all scale together as \((1+z)\)
[5]. Then \(e^2\propto(1+z)^2\) gives \(\alpha\propto(1+z)^2\) — a fine-structure
constant bounded to parts in \(10^5\)
across the observable universe acquires a factor of order the redshift
squared — and the Bohr radius runs as \[a_0 =
\frac{\hbar^2}{m e^2} \propto (1+z)^{-3},\] atoms
smaller in the past by three powers while the theory’s own
redshift requires them larger by one: a miss of \((1+z)^4\). The assignment is not an
unclosed dictionary that further work might close; it is the inverse of
the unique closed one.

2.2The rigid rod

The failure above has a one-line cause worth isolating, because it is
the single dictionary entry that cannot be scaled away: \[a_0\, m \;=\; \frac{\hbar}{\alpha c}.
\tag{1}\] With \(\hbar\),
\(c\) and \(\alpha\) held fixed — and every laboratory
constraint says the right-hand side is fixed to high precision — the
product of atomic size and particle mass is a rigid rod. Size and mass
must scale oppositely. Any proposal in which matter’s size and
matter’s mass shrink together is not a transformation of units at all;
it changes the rod, and the rod is measured. One is free to move the
scale of matter, but only along the one-parameter family Eq. (1) permits.

2.3The fork: reframing or environment

Once the dictionary is closed, a theory of matter at a scale faces an
exhaustive choice.

Branch 1: pure reframing. The scaling is a global function
of epoch, identical for all matter everywhere. Then the construction is
a conformal change of frame, its prediction set is identical to the
standard one, and it is honest to say so: zero new predictions, zero new
conflicts. This is a defensible and publishable position — it is
Wetterich’s — and its value is conceptual: it exhibits the gauge freedom
in what cosmology treats as fixed.

Branch 2: environment dependence. The scaling rate depends
on where the matter sits — on local density, on the “mean emptiness”
between masses. Then the construction is no longer a reframing. It is a
scalar–tensor theory with a matter coupling, and it meets the constraint
wall as one:

A theory cannot occupy both branches: claiming immunity from all

levels. The choice is internal to the theory, costs no experiment, and
everything downstream depends on it. Sec. 5 executes the
one confrontation Branch 2 has left.

2.4The mechanism does not close, and does not need
to

The proposed engine — all moving mass radiates gravitational waves,
hence all matter loses momentum, hence all matter shrinks — fails twice.
Gravitational radiation requires changing multipole moments, and an
isolated stationary system — an atom included — has none; and even where
radiation occurs, radiated energy changes orbital dynamics, and
no derivation connects that loss to a change in rest masses or atomic
length scales. The bridge from radiative loss to shrinking matter is
never built. The honest repair costs little: state the scaling as a
postulate — Branch 1 needs no mechanism, being a choice of frame — and
let the phenomenology carry the theory.

2.5What the observations actually close

Cosmological time dilation is now detected in both supernova light
curves and quasar variability — Lewis and Brewer monitored 190 quasars
over two decades and found the \((1+z)\) dilation [12] — which closes the static and
tired-light models that cannot reproduce the \((1+z)\) temporal dilation — a class, though
not every conceivable static construction. A shrinking-matter theory
with a closed dictionary survives this specific test, because shrinking
clocks carry the dilation. It is worth saying explicitly which
neighbouring positions are dead, so the surviving one is not buried with
them.

3Matter at a scale as a theorem

We now transplant the demand into a setting where it can be decided.
The operator framework of [6] builds its observables from a finite-rank
operator \(K\): spectral projectors,
principal angles between occupied subspaces, and a boundary channel
census \(N\) counting the eigenvalues
of \(\mathbf{1}_A P \mathbf{1}_A\)
lying in \([\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\). The question
“is there an absolute reference of scale?” becomes exact: which
observables survive the offset \(K\mapsto
K+cI\) and the dilation \(K\mapsto
cK\)?

3.1Zero

The offset is settled by the Baseline Operator Theorem of [6]: all difference observables
are invariant under \(K\mapsto K+cI\).
There is no absolute reference of offset; an instrument reads only
differences, and the tare term separates exactly.
[T]\(^{[6]}\) —
imported; the proof lives in [6] and is not reproduced here.

3.2Span

The dilation splits into layers.


Lemma 1 (Span-blindness of the subspace layer).
Spectral projectors and principal angles are invariant under \(K\mapsto cK\) for all \(c>0\). (Negative \(c\) reverses spectral order, so a projector
defined as an occupied sector can map to its complement; positive
dilations are the span transformations meant throughout.)



The proof is one line — eigenvectors do not move under a global
rescale — and the lemma is stated as a lemma precisely so that nothing
is claimed from it: it is the reason the framework’s
observables have the property the shrinking-matter tradition postulates,
without anyone postulating it. Verified to \(4\times10^{-15}\) across twelve orders of
magnitude in \(c\) [6]. [T]\(^{[6]}\)


Theorem 3 (Infrared span invariance). For a
gapped substrate with correlation length \(\xi\), the boundary census \(N\) is independent of the infrared region
size: measured flat (\(N=4\)) as the
region grows from \(L_A/\xi = 6.3\) to
\(126\).



This is the statement that makes an area law legitimate: the census
reads the boundary and nothing else, and no observable in the list
carries an absolute infrared length. [V]\(^{[6]}\)

The dilation-covariant quantities are exactly the dimensionful ones —
\(\|K\|_F\sim c\), the Henrici stress
functional \(\sim c^4\) — and the
dilation-invariant list is exactly the dimensionless ratios: relative
departure from normality, eigenvector condition numbers, spectral-radius
ratios. Only ratios are observables, and every ratio is span-free.

3.3The anomaly

The demand fails in exactly one place, and the failure has structure.
In the ultraviolet the census is not scale-free: it runs logarithmically
in the ratio of correlation length to cutoff, \[N \;=\; |\partial A|\cdot
\frac{c}{\pi^2}\,\log\!\frac{\xi}{a}\,
\log\!\frac{1}{\varepsilon} \;+\; \text{subleading},
\tag{2}\] per boundary point, where \(c\) is the central charge of the substrate.
The coefficient is a theorem: equal spacing of the entanglement ladder,
the exact integral \(\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
H(\nu(\epsilon))\,d\epsilon =
\pi^2/3\), the level-density bridge \(S=(\pi^2/6)\,dN/d\Lambda\), and the
Calabrese–Cardy block entropy [13], with the finite-size mechanism
supplied in generality by the boundary-CFT correspondence of [14] (\(L\to\ln(\xi/a)\), integrability not
required). Measured at \(c=1\): \(0.202692\) against \(2/\pi^2=0.202642\) for a two-endpoint
interval, a distance of \(4.9\times10^{-5}\) [6]. [T]\(^{[6]}\)+[V]\(^{[6]}\)


Remark 1 (Exact where it should be, broken where it
must be). Scale invariance holds exactly wherever the observable is
a pure ratio, and is broken only where a cutoff ratio \(\xi/a\) enters. That is the structure of an
anomaly, not of a defect — the same structure quantum scale symmetry has
at a fixed point [4], which places Wetterich on both sides
of this paper: the rigorous member of the shrinking-matter lineage, and
the name of the symmetry whose anomalous breaking the census
exhibits.




Remark 2 (A rescaling can only be seen as a
running). Suppose the substrate of the universe were rescaled. Every
projector, every angle, every infrared count is blind to it; the only
quantity that moves is the coefficient of a logarithm. An observer
inside the system cannot register “everything is now half the size” as a
fact; they can only register a drift in a running. Within this operator
census — for the scale-blind observable class just listed, not as a
universal claim about all physical observables — a change of scale is
observationally a renormalization-group statement, never a power law;
which is sharper than the original equivalence claim: it says not only
that absolute size is unobservable, but exactly what the residue of a
size change would look like. [H]



3.4The coefficient carries the central charge: the Ising
test

Equation (2)’s only falsifiable
dependence is the central charge. The substrates that produced \(2/\pi^2\) both carry one complex fermion,
\(c=1\). The transverse-field Ising
chain is half a fermion — one Majorana, \(c=\tfrac12\) — so the theorem demands the
block-entropy slope drop from \(\tfrac13\) to \(\tfrac16\) and the interval coefficient
from \(2/\pi^2\) to \(1/\pi^2\): a factor of two between
hypotheses, with no fit ambiguity.

The test is one self-contained gate (script ISING-A-03,
Appendix A, which prints every number quoted here): exact block
entropies from the Majorana covariance matrix on rings up to \(65{,}536\) sites, on the disordered side,
with \(\xi=1/\ln h\) carried from \(5.5\) to \(800.5\) and the instrument control shown at
every point (\(\xi\cdot\mathrm{gap}\to2\) as \(h\to1\), the gap being \(2(h-1)\) exactly). Two estimators are
declared in the script before the data: the raw tail slope \(E_1\) over the last four points (\(\xi=200\)–\(800\)), which finite-\(\xi\) corrections push above the
asymptote, and a three-parameter \(1/\ln\xi\)-corrected fit \(E_2\), which at finite range lands
below it. To close the window-choice question, \(E_2\) is reported over three windows —
\(\xi\ge20\), \(\xi\ge50\), and the same tail window \(\xi\ge200\) as \(E_1\) — and the bracket claim requires it
below \(\tfrac16\) in all of them. The
gate returns \[E_1 = 0.170562,\qquad E_2 =
0.158564,\ 0.158987,\ 0.160840,\] bracketing the prediction \(\tfrac16=0.166667\) from opposite sides in
every window, and a measured coefficient \[A_{\rm total} = 0.103689 \quad\text{against}\quad
1/\pi^2 = 0.101321,\] \(41.8\)
times closer to the \(c=\tfrac12\)
prediction than to the \(c=1\) value
\(2/\pi^2=0.202642\). Under the gate’s
pre-stated verdict rule — the window-robust bracket plus a factor-five
distance criterion — the hypothesis test passes: the coefficient of the
running carries the central charge. [V]

Stated honestly, and printed by the gate itself: the raw estimator
carries a finite-\(\xi\) excess of
\(+0.0039\) above \(\tfrac16\) — halved by the last doubling of
the lever arm, with the increments of \(S-\tfrac16\ln\xi\) per doubling themselves
shrinking (\(+0.0035\to+0.0020\)) — so
the coefficient is confirmed at the two-percent level with every
correction moving toward the prediction. Closing the residual to the
\(10^{-5}\) level of the \(c=1\) case remains a longer-lever-arm job.
What is settled is the factor-of-two question, which is the test.

4What matter is

If nothing about matter carries an absolute size, the difference
between matter and gas — and between either and vacuum — must be
statable entirely in scale-free quantities. In the operator framework it
is, and the statement is short: matter is a gapped substrate wearing
a boundary.

4.1The gap is the difference

The census of Sec. 3 behaves in two qualitatively
different ways, both measured [6]. Over a gapped substrate it is
finite, reads the boundary alone, and obeys an area law: \(N\) per boundary site is order one, flat in
the infrared, running only through Eq. (2). Over a gapless
substrate the census floods: \(N\)
grows as \(L\log L\) — the
Gioev–Klich–Widom violation of the area law [15], observed directly as \(N/4L\) climbing from \(1.25\) to \(2.07\) with system size — and no finite
budget exists at any threshold. [V]

Read as physics, that dichotomy is the matter/gas distinction.
Opening a gap is what converts an unbounded, free directional budget
into a finite ledger with prices: in the gapped phase a direction costs
one boundary channel, angular resolution costs census depth, and the
budget can be watched binding [7]. In the gapless phase, in the class studied,
channels are plentiful, directions cost nothing, and the finite boundary
budget of the gapped phase does not exist — and since every protection
result in this framework is a budget statement, nothing there is
protected in the framework’s sense. The shorter reading — gas has no
budget — is kept as interpretation. [H] Condensation,
in this language, is not primarily a density change; it is the
appearance of the gap that makes the boundary ledger finite.

Gas is also distinguished dynamically — and this paragraph is the
operator language reading physical systems it does not derive, an
analogy marked as one. A gas at rest sits on the normal manifold:
symmetric and skew parts commute, the pseudospectrum is a disc around
each eigenvalue, and the spectrum is the whole story. A gas under shear
is the founding example of the opposite regime — every eigenvalue
damped, transition to turbulence anyway [16, 17] — so the
characteristic failure mode of gas is non-modal: transient
growth from non-orthogonal modes, invisible to the spectrum, terminating
in a nonlinear trigger. Condensed matter fails at spectral walls; gas
fails pseudospectrally. And gas has no walls of its own until it
manufactures them: driven shear layers and crowded crossings grow their
own rigidity interfaces in the interior, and the response localizes onto
the self-made wall — measured at the \(99\%\) level in a lattice transport model
[7] and observed as the
confinement of turbulence to self-generated lanes in solar granulation
[18]. Gas is the
wall-free medium that builds walls under load.

The cosmic arena for all of this is real and instrumented: roughly
half the present-day baryons are gas in the warm–hot intergalactic
medium at \(10^5\)–\(10^7\) K. Its temperature is measured
independently of any dynamical model by X-ray spectroscopy — stacked
eROSITA filament spectra give \(\log_{10}(T/\mathrm{K}) = 6.84 \pm 0.07\)
[19] — and its ionized
baryon content is counted independently through fast radio burst
dispersion [20]. Any
environment-dependent scaling claim about gas — including the proposal
that blue-shifted intergalactic gas requires no heat source — confronts
those instruments directly.

4.2Three ratios classify

Standard physics classifies matter by constituents and symmetry. The
operator framework classifies it by three numbers, each a span-free
ratio, so that the classification itself obeys the no-absolute-scale
demand:

(i) Distance from normality, and what maintains it. The
relative departure from normality \(\Delta_F^2/\|K\|_F^2\) is
dilation-invariant. Gas at rest sits at zero. A star is matter held
off the normal manifold by throughput — equilibrium would be a
normal operator, and the core prevents it, exporting the misalignment at
a rate that is the entropy production. A black hole is matter pinned at
a wall that never answers. Three phases, one axis, no absolute scale on
it.

(ii) Census occupancy against the geometric cap. Whether the
boundary budget binds is a ratio of two counts. Gapless gas: unbound at
every threshold. Gapped matter: bound, area-law, priced. The crossover
is measurable in one sweep [7].

(iii) Which records cross the walls. Metric-protected
amplitude dies at a rigidity wall as \(1/\sqrt{2\lambda}\); integer-protected
phase crosses. What a piece of matter is, durably, is the set
of its integer records — windings, signatures, channel counts — because
those are the quantities that survive its own boundaries. Amplitude is
the part of matter that its walls consume; the integers are the part
that persists.

Together with Eq. (1) these give the paper’s answer to
its own question. Matter has no absolute size and needs none: it is a
gap, a boundary, a held distance from equilibrium, and a set of integers
— every one of them a ratio or a whole number — with a single logarithm,
Eq. (2), keeping honest account of
the one place scale sneaks back in. [H]

5The confrontation, executed

The environmental branch of Sec. 2.3 — dark
matter as a \(G\) that varies with the
mean emptiness between masses — is the original contribution of [5], and it is directly
testable. Desmond and Stiskalek–Desmond find no secondary correlations
in the full SPARC sample [21, 22]; the 2026 identifiability audit
[24] measures the
extraction boundary — any genuine secondary parameter must clear a \(0.106\) dex per-galaxy nuisance floor, of
which \(0.080\) dex is absorbable
scatter — and finds one regime retaining structural information:
gas-dominated, low-acceleration disks, where the RAR residual correlates
positively with compactness (\(r=0.46\), \(p=1.3\times10^{-4}\); hierarchical partial
pooling \(\beta=0.23\), \(N=63\)). It is fitting that the informative
regime is the gas phase of Sec. 4.1.

The confrontation is run against the residual the data actually
report (script SPARC-SIGN-03, Appendix A). The audit’s
residual is \(\Delta=\log_{10}g_{\rm
obs}-\log_{10}g_{\rm RAR}(g_{\rm bar})\) with the McGaugh
interpolating function [23]; it differs from the Newtonian
variable-\(G\) residual \(\log_{10}(g_{\rm obs}/g_{\rm bar})\) by the
baseline boost \(B(g_{\rm
bar})=\log_{10}(g_{\rm RAR}/g_{\rm bar})\), which runs from \(0.57\) dex at the regime’s defining split
boundary (\(\log_{10}g_{\rm
bar}=-10.926\)) to \(1.06\) dex
at its faint end — the term a naive identification drops, restored here.
Under the minimal monotone mechanism \(G_{\rm
eff}/G_N=1+\eta E\), with \(E\)
the normalized emptiness, \[\Delta \;=\;
\log_{10}(1+\eta E)\;-\;B(g_{\rm bar}),\] and the mechanism has
exactly two ways to inhabit the data. If it is the origin of the
dark-matter boost, self-consistency with the empirical relation fixes
the conditional log-mean, \(\langle\log_{10}(1+\eta E)\,|\,g_{\rm bar}\rangle
= B(g_{\rm bar})\) — equivalently, a conditional geometric mean
of \(1+\eta E\) between \(3.7\) at the split boundary and \(11.5\) at the faint end — and the residual
becomes the fluctuation of emptiness about its acceleration-conditional
mean, with compactness derivative \[\frac{d\Delta}{d\log_{10}C}\Big|_{g_{\rm bar},M}
\;=\; -\,\frac{\eta E}{1+\eta E}\,\frac{d\ln E}{d\ln
C}\;<\;0\] for every \(\eta>0\) and every \(E\) decreasing in \(C\): compact galaxies below their
acceleration-mates, diffuse ones above. The second branch is a
continuum: to the extent \(E\) carries
variation independent of \((g_{\rm
bar},M)\), the negative signature above applies to that
component; to the extent it does not, \(G(\text{emptiness})\) reduces to an
acceleration-dependent \(G\) — a
reformulation of the acceleration relation itself, in which the
emptiness story carries no independent, testable content in this data
and any residual independent component is bounded by the extraction
floor. A nearly redundant \(E\) buys no
escape: it trades the sign falsification for the admission that
emptiness adds nothing the relation lacked. Within the minimal monotone
model class \(G_{\rm eff}/G_N=1+\eta
E\) there is no third assignment.

The measurements oppose the first branch three separate ways [24]: the regime-local
correlation is positive (\(r=+0.46\),
\(\beta=+0.23\), \(N=63\)) — the direct sign measurement —
while two full-sample quantities point the same, direction-consistent
way: the extreme-compactness decile means put diffuse galaxies
\(0.29\) dex below the
relation with compact ones on it (\(-0.02\)), and the audit’s fitted modulation
(\(\varepsilon=-0.077\)) suppresses the
most diffuse systems. Every measured sign is the opposite of
emptiness-driven \(G\). And the audit’s
honest alternative reading — that the positive signal is unmodelled
pressure support — closes the remaining door from the other side: then
the regime holds no positive evidence for any secondary parameter at
all, and the mechanism must live below the \(0.106\) dex per-galaxy nuisance floor
(\(0.080\) dex of it absorbable
scatter), where it does no work. A \(G\) that grows with emptiness gets nothing
from the one regime that retains structural information.
[V]\(_{\rm lit}\)

The scope of this exclusion is deliberately narrow, and narrower is
stronger: the claim is not that galaxy dynamics admits no environmental
dependence, but that the specific monotone emptiness-driven \(G\) is incompatible with the sign of the
one structurally informative regime. The baseline it confronts is not an
arbitrary noisy relation — the RAR’s observed tightness is at the
few-hundredths-of-a-dex level under standard SPARC modelling [31] — while the caution that
SPARC’s dynamic range limits what the relation’s exact functional form
can establish [30] cuts
the other way and is inherited: the confrontation here is conditional on
the empirical baseline, not on its uniqueness.

The constructive residue: the surviving positive correlation, if it
is physics at all, points at a quantity that grows with density
— which is not a Casimir-emptiness mechanism. A revised proposal must
supply a reason the sign reverses, or take Branch 1 and release the
dark-matter claim, or wait out the pressure-support question the audit
leaves open — and the third option merely defers the choice between the
first two. Two anchors for further work: the Bullet Cluster version of
the claim is quantitatively a lensing surface-density map, and an
acceleration scale that runs with epoch is now under active measurement,
with intermediate-redshift rotation-curve samples preferring a larger
\(a_0\) than SPARC at \(z=0\) [25] — a model-dependent inference, flagged as
such, but exactly the observable a running-scale theory speaks to, in
either direction.

6Conclusion

The demand that there be no absolute reference of size is correct,
and taken seriously it earns its keep four times over. As a units claim
it closes uniquely into Wetterich’s dictionary, with the Bohr rod \(a_0m=\hbar/(\alpha c)\) fixing the one
direction the scaling cannot go. As a physical theory it bifurcates, and
the environmental branch’s confrontation is now executed against the
residual the data actually report: its predicted sign is opposite the
only surviving candidate signal, and every escape either collapses it to
a reframing or strands it below the extraction floor. As a statement
about observables it is a theorem for the zero, a theorem and a measured
fact for the span, and false by exactly one logarithm at the cutoff — a
logarithm whose coefficient is derived, confirmed at \(5\times10^{-5}\) at \(c=1\), and now tested at \(c=\tfrac12\), where it passes the
factor-of-two hypothesis test it was built to face. And as a question —
if matter has no absolute size, what is it? — it answers itself: matter
is a gap wearing a boundary. A finite ledger where gas has none; a held
distance from equilibrium where gas sits at zero; a set of integer
records where gas keeps only flux. Every classifier a ratio or a whole
number, and the single exception a running whose coefficient is a
central charge.

Matter at a scale is possible up to that running. Where the demand
holds, it was never in danger; where it fails, the failure has a
coefficient, the coefficient has a central charge in it, and the central
charge is now tested at two values of \(c\). The interesting physics lives where
the demand fails.

def binary_H(x):
    x = np.clip(x, 1e-15, 1 - 1e-15)
    return -(x*np.log(x) + (1-x)*np.log(1-x))

def block_entropy_tfim(h, L, N):
    """Exact block entropy of L sites in an N-site TFIM ring at field h (J=1)."""
    k = 2*np.pi*(np.arange(N) + 0.5)/N            # antiperiodic (NS) sector
    eps = np.sqrt((h - np.cos(k))**2 + np.sin(k)**2)
    expphi = ((h - np.cos(k)) - 1j*np.sin(k))/eps
    idx0 = L
    l = np.arange(-L, L+1)
    gr = np.array([(expphi*np.exp(1j*k*ll)).sum().real/N for ll in l])
    G = np.zeros((2*L, 2*L))
    ar = np.arange(L)
    for m in range(L):
        G[2*m, 1::2] = gr[idx0 - m : idx0 - m + L]
        G[2*m + 1, 0::2] = -gr[idx0 + m - ar]
    w = np.linalg.eigvalsh(1j*G)
    nu = np.sort(np.abs(w))[::2][::-1]
    return binary_H((1+nu)/2).sum()

#!/usr/bin/env python3
# SCRIPT: LADDER-C12-01  (supersedes CENSUS-2D-01/02: the counting instrument was

# WHY: link 1 of the coefficient theorem's chain is the EQUALLY SPACED entanglement

# pre-stated bars: (a) UNIFORMITY -- the single-particle entanglement energies are odd

# measured ladder reconstructs the independently measured block entropy S to <= 1%,
# so the ladder is the whole entropy story; (c) the eps-window census is linear in
# Lambda = ln((1-eps)/eps) with uniform rung residuals <= one doublet. What this gate
# does NOT claim: an absolute census normalization across substrate conventions -- the

def ladder(h, Nring, mult=6):
    xi = 1/np.log(h); L = int(mult*xi)
    k = 2*np.pi*(np.arange(Nring)+0.5)/Nring
    e = np.sqrt((h-np.cos(k))**2 + np.sin(k)**2)

def block_cov(h, Nring, mult=6):
    xi = 1/np.log(h); L = int(mult*xi)
    k = 2*np.pi*(np.arange(Nring)+0.5)/Nring
    e = np.sqrt((h-np.cos(k))**2 + np.sin(k)**2)
    expphi = ((h-np.cos(k)) - 1j*np.sin(k))/e

        G[2*m+1, 0::2] = -gr[idx0+m-ar]
    return G

    w = np.linalg.eigvalsh(1j*G)
    nu = np.sort(np.abs(w))[::2][::-1]
