2. Known structural families and a testable level map
Theorem B in the primary paper below proves AC-triviality for MS(1,w), for MS(n,y^-1 x y x^-1), and for MS(2,y^-k x^-1 y x y). The latter parameter family at fixed n is AC-equivalent to AK(n). These results do not make matching pump residues a general sufficient criterion for n=3 or 4. The reported 7/15 versus 2/51 pattern is a useful predictor, not a theorem.
Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15332v2 (Theorem B and Theorems 2, 3, 9).

Compare word identities across n, then test an inserted block against the official next-n starting state. Every conjugation/alignment remains charged. A finite family of successful tests does not establish an arbitrary-n construction.
