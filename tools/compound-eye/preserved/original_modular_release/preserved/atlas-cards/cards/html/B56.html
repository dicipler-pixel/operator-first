B56L1 → L3 theorem

A curve and a point do not meet.

det g = 0 : one real equation    coalescence : two
codimension one against codimension two — settled before any model

In two real parameters the vanishing of the determinant is a single equation and cuts a curve, while an eigenvalue coalescence is two equations and cuts isolated points. They do not generically intersect. Only the geometric locus has two sides, which is what “wall” was always reaching for — and it is codimension one only once the form can go indefinite, which a Hermitian system cannot do.
