A picture should carry the conditions of its own use.

That is the next role for the Atlas: each rule names its interface, allowed move, retained information, readout and proof. B94 specifies how an error is measured. B95 distinguishes a spectral count from geometric packing. B96 asks for actual outward and return maps before a loop can be evaluated.

The finite replacement shown here is exact. Extending it to the physical Offset operators, uniform sofic filling bounds or a phase–count energy balance remains mathematical work to do. This demonstration adds no new Lean theorem.

Built on established Schur-complement identities and the principles of compositional linear networks and contextual equivalence. The proposed contribution is an Atlas language whose diagrams retain the information its actual questions require.
