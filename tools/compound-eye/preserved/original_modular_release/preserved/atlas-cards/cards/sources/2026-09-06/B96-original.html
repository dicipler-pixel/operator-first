The gap between the rails is the supply.

out on the phase  ·  back on the count  ·  Δ around the loop
the offset driven to zero at every size, or an offset no enlargement removes

Light is the high-side reference: massless, one speed, no rest frame, so it cannot drift and everything is measured against it. Matter is the low-side return: it has a rest frame, it can be held, and it forms and evaporates. Nothing is measured against one rail alone — the potential exists because the two run at different rates, which is why the ledger lives on the high side and the census on the low. A finite approximation is a completed circuit: out along the phase, through a body that will not survive, back along the count, closing on the reference it started from. The whole question is whether that return lands.
