# Projector susceptibility and the separating gap

Stable ID: `UPG-GAP-RANK`

## Statement

[H,dot(P)]=[P,dot(H)]; cross-cluster derivative entries have denominators equal to eigenvalue separations.

## Assumptions

Differentiable Hermitian family with an isolated selected cluster.

## Failure mode

An eigenvalue passing zero need not close a cluster gap; a full-rank operator can have a degenerate selected cluster.

## Evidence status

Written spectral derivative plus independent finite-difference and refusal controls

## Verification scope

See research/upg/verification.json and integration report; original UPG gates are numerical, and paper interpretations are not certified by them.

## Credit

Jeromie N. Beasley research program; standard projector, Schur, spectral and knot methods retain their original attribution. No broad novelty claim.

## Next step

Connect a calibrated electrical response to the chosen H and its parameter derivative.
