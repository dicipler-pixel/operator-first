# Four records of a reduction

Stable ID: `CASCADE-FOUR-RECORDS`

## Statement

Track support, projector motion, hidden feedback and a separately named topological invariant during a cascade.

## Assumptions

Each record carries its own operator, units, boundary and evidence stage.

## Failure mode

Covariance overlap is not dynamical memory; an energy descent is not automatically work; a window count can increase under a positive peel.

## Evidence status

Instrument architecture plus preserved QHE and cascade controls

## Verification scope

See research/upg/verification.json and integration report; original UPG gates are numerical, and paper interpretations are not certified by them.

## Credit

Jeromie N. Beasley research program; standard projector, Schur, spectral and knot methods retain their original attribution. No broad novelty claim.

## Next step

Specify the UPG feature map, measured response, independent splits and energy costs for the next acquisition test.
