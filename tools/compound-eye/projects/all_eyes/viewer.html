Reading this instrument
Recorded sweeps contain actual executed Python outputs. Live mode calls the registered Python eyes at the value you choose. The QHE panel replays existing classified acquisition counts; the prime panel reprocesses existing exact sums. These coordinates are setting indices and prime counts, not physical time.
Traces share a coordinate only within the selected study. Overlay scaling maps each selected trace to its own recorded range; it does not add unlike physical units. Two-readout trajectories use their displayed raw scales. A visual match or correlation is a lead to test, and eyes that share a kernel are dependent evidence.
Complete requests, results, input mappings and hashes are in the accompanying runnable package. There is no live laboratory connection. Nuclear acquisition and the 17 specified methods still require their listed inputs or constructions.
