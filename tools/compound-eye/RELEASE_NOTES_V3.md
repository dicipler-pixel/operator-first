- Preserves every prior eye version, set, installed implementation, Atlas entry, dataset and project.
- Adds eleven scoped inside/outside, metric, continuation and observation views; 178 total eye versions, 27 sets.

- Adds a dark standalone boundary lab, comparison, full manual, source audit, exact symbolic checks and result records.
- Records the finite signed wall path, quotient-metric self-adjoint transport, regular lifted limit, oscillator normalization correction and explicit observational ambiguity.
- Adds the new suite to run_all.py. Existing scientific eye implementations are unchanged.

The original top-level portal and instructions remain in preserved/universal_2_portal/. Any changed original archive entries are separately retained with byte hashes. The current papers are unchanged. New physics, raw observational fits and Lean formalization remain open as detailed in the report.
