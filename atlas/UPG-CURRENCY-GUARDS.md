# Winding, signature and eta remain distinct

Stable ID: `UPG-CURRENCY-GUARDS`

## Statement

Finite commutator winding is integer-valued away from its log branch; knot signature plateaus can cancel despite Alexander roots.

## Assumptions

Unitary relator with explicit branch guard; separate knot Seifert form and holonomy conventions.

## Failure mode

Dimension-two clock-shift closes the branch. A knot signature is not automatically the minimal unit record or an APS eta invariant.

## Evidence status

Source winding replay and independent signature cancellation control

## Verification scope

See research/upg/verification.json and integration report; original UPG gates are numerical, and paper interpretations are not certified by them.

## Credit

Jeromie N. Beasley research program; standard projector, Schur, spectral and knot methods retain their original attribution. No broad novelty claim.

## Next step

Specify manifold, operator and representation before testing an eta/CS/torsion bridge.
