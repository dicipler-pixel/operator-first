# Elemental spacing and probe-dependent channels

Stable ID: `ELEMENTAL-SPACING-COUPLING`

## Statement

The fixed-gap electronic-sheet Hamiltonian has optical strength sin²θ and, for the declared leads at E=0, G/G₀=γ²a²sin²θ/(a²+γ²/4)². Atomic and optical elemental comparisons remain distinct observations.

## Assumptions

Two-level model, fixed dipole and contact orientations, elastic wide-band leads; optical data and continuum film assumptions are separately stated.

## Failure mode

Energy spacing alone does not identify transition strength; atomic ionization thresholds are not conducting-solid band gaps.

## Evidence status

Written derivation and fresh numerical checks; no new Lean compilation

## Verification scope

Seven new eyes, 1,253 evaluations, four expected refusals and 846 assertions; recorded NIST/optical data replay. No measured atom-by-atom cascade.

## Credit

Recovered user hypersurface/color programme; standard Landauer, Maxwell and CIE methods; NIST, Johnson–Christy and Rakić sources attributed in the supplement.
