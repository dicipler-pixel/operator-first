# Two-frequency silent-subspace test

Stable ID: `OPT-TWO-FREQUENCY`

## Statement

In the finite lossless model, the divided response difference in a pole-free interval has the metric kernel. Two exact scalar zeros for one real direction imply zero coupling.

## Assumptions

Fixed positive gaps and positive residues, fixed transition numerators, real direction, distinct squared photon energies, no crossed pole.

## Failure mode

Crossed poles, signed residues or changed response model can invalidate the implication; finite error bounds give a bound, not exact darkness.
