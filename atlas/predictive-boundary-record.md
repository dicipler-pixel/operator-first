# Identical reduced observations can conceal opposite response slopes

Stable ID: `predictive-boundary-record`

## Statement

A specified two-orbital pair has equal initial conductance, noise, isolated optical transition data and full squared-angle compression, but opposite transmission slopes and distinct graph projectors.

## Assumptions

Fixed two-orbital Hamiltonian family, wide-band contacts, physical reference basis, noninteracting coherent scattering and the declared isolated optical probe.

## Failure mode

A single reduced snapshot cannot distinguish the pair. Arbitrary future response cannot be reconstructed from one full scattering snapshot without a model either.

## Evidence status

Written exact proof; 550 registered evaluations plus 340 source controls. Formal status is recorded by exact source revision in the project.

## Verification scope

Finite mathematics, domain controls and model computations; elemental sources are calibrations.

## Credit

Classical Feshbach, Fano and graph-projector identities; Beasley source manuscripts supply the observation architecture. The combined benchmark makes its discrimination claim exact.

## Next step

Calibrated held-out physical prediction with recorded geometry, phase reference and intervention.
